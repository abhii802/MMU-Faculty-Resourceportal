Resources 04–06

Document Generators

The blank page is the hardest part of any paper, deck, or set of notes. These three tools take a single topic and hand back a structured first draft — a starting document, not a finished one. What goes out under a faculty member's name is still their own writing, checked, corrected, and completed by them.

04 Needs backend link

Research Paper Draft Generator

Enter a topic and receive a structured DOCX draft — abstract, introduction, literature review, methodology, results, discussion, and references — as a starting point for the real writing and review that follows.

05 Needs backend link

PPT Draft Generator

Enter a topic and receive a structured PPTX outline — ready to be re-timed, restyled, and taught from, rather than built slide-by-slide from a blank deck.

06 Needs backend link

Notes Draft Generator

Enter a topic and receive a structured DOCX set of notes — organised by concept, ready to be edited into the exact notes a class will be taught from.

i

A note on Resources 04–06

These three run through a private backend service — a topic goes in, a DOCX or PPTX comes out. The forms above are wired and ready; they just need the service's URL entered in GENERATOR_ENDPOINTS near the bottom of this page's source, once that service is deployed. The scripts themselves stay in a private repo and never appear on this page.

The paper generator is the slowest of the three — it fetches references and builds figures before the file is ready, so a wait of a minute or two there is expected, not a fault.

About MMDU, Mullana

Maharishi Markandeshwar (Deemed to be University), Mullana–Ambala grew out of the Maharishi Markandeshwar Educational Trust, founded to widen access to higher education across engineering, medicine, dentistry, nursing, pharmacy, management, and law. The Mullana campus in Haryana is one of the larger multi-disciplinary campuses in the region, bringing several professional streams together under one university.

The Department of Computer Science & Engineering at MM Engineering College sits within that campus — teaching, mentoring, and research running in parallel, which is exactly the kind of week this console exists to make lighter.
